MulliganPass & play

A little golf. A little mischief.

Two to four players. One phone. Plan in private, then swing together.

  1. Choose two different cards. Use one as your club and the other as an action. Each physical card has both faces, but you can only use one face at a time.
  2. Pick a target. Help yourself or interfere with another player. Lock your shot and pass the phone.
  3. Reveal together. When everyone has chosen, see all shots. Pass the phone once more so each player can use a Mulligan to cancel an action or an earlier cancellation.
  4. Swing. All shots resolve together. Your hand refills to eight. Keep going until everyone finishes, then move to the next hole. Lowest total strokes wins; ties share the win.

Getting to the hole

Driver is for your first shot only. Land exactly on the hole to finish, or get within 25 yards and use a 1-Putt next time. 1-Putt ignores all actions. Overshooting leaves you the excess distance to play back toward the hole.

Special cards

Hands and both piles carry over between holes. Refill to eight after shots; shuffle only the current discard pile when the draw pile runs out.

Choose Human or an Easy, Normal, Hard or Expert CPU for each seat. CPUs use their own cards and public information only. Discarded cards cannot be inspected by humans or CPUs. CPU names are assigned automatically. With CPUs, shared screens advance automatically until a human needs to act. Use Pause autoplay to review a screen. Expert may remember cards revealed during play, but cannot inspect discarded or hidden cards.

Gold “Play now” cards work before you lock a shot. Dig exchanges cards, Borrow lets you choose a card from another hand, and Read the lie reveals a locked shot. Mulligan works during the reaction pass. Undo last Mulligan returns your most recent choice to your hand until you pass or finish reactions. None can be used as a face-down action.

If you need a new hand, take a practice swing: exchange your hand instead of playing a shot, at the cost of one stroke. Each hole has a 12-shot limit; unfinished players pick up at at least par +8.

House edition: what is reconstructed?

The official deck has 104 club/action cards. This edition uses verified card examples and simultaneous play, with an inferred card distribution, eight-card hands, stroke-play scoring, deterministic stacking (yard adjustments, then halving), and fixed reaction windows for a shared phone. Clear fairway, Read the lie, and the specific Mulligan cancellation rule are house implementations. This is an independent adaptation, not an official digital release.

Save & install

Your round saves on this browser after each move. Reloading covers private hands. Use your mobile browser’s “Add to Home Screen” or “Install app” menu. After the first full load, the game can work offline. Clearing browser data erases local saves. Saves do not sync between phones.
